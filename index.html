Welcome to Harbor Light Books & Stationery

Harbor Light Books & Stationery is an independent Bangor bookstore for readers of all ages.

Our shelves include fiction, mystery, nonfiction, children's books, Maine authors, journals, stationery, and literary gifts.

We also host readings, story programs, book discussions, and community events that bring readers and writers together.

About Us

Harbor Light Books & Stationery is an independent neighborhood bookstore serving readers, families, students, teachers, local writers, and gift buyers.

The bookstore combines a thoughtfully selected book collection with stationery, gifts, and community programming.

Our Services

Book Recommendations

Our knowledgeable booksellers help customers discover books that match their interests and reading needs.

Special Orders

Customers can contact the store about books that are not currently in stock to ask whether a special order can be placed.

Community Events

The bookstore hosts author readings, children's story programs, book discussions, and events for local writers.
