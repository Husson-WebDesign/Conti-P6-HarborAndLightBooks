Stories, Stationery, and Shared Moments

Step inside to explore a carefully curated selection of fine journals, stationery, literary gifts as well as, fiction, mystery, and other works by Maine authors.

Beyond the shelves, we are a gathering place where readers and writers come together for readings, story programs, book discussions, and community events.

Whether you are searching for your next great page-turner or looking for a cozy place to spend the afternoon, our friendly staff is always here to help you find something special. Grab a warm cup of tea, browse our hand-picked staff recommendations, and become a part of our growing local literary family. Come visit us today and see where your next adventure begins!
