About Us

Nestled right in the heart of Bangor, Harbor Light Books & Stationery was created to be a warm, welcoming gathering place for everyone who loves a good story. Our purpose goes beyond simply selling books, we strive to cultivate a cozy, inspiring space where curiosity is sparked, conversations begin, and neighbors can slow down and discover something wonderful.

As an independent, neighborhood-focused shop, we take pride in serving a vibrant community of readers, families, students, local writers, and thoughtful gift buyers. Our shelves feature a thoughtfully curated collection of books spanning diverse genres, alongside a carefully chosen selection of fine stationery, unique gifts, and creative supplies. More than just a retail space, we are deeply committed to connecting with our community through engaging events, author visits, and programming that brings people together.
