About Harbor Light

Nestled right in the heart of Bangor, Harbor Light Books & Stationery was created to be a warm, welcoming gathering place for everyone who loves a good story. Our purpose goes beyond simply selling books, we strive to cultivate a cozy, inspiring space where curiosity is sparked, conversations begin, and neighbors can slow down and discover something wonderful. Harbor Light Books & Stationery is a fictional independent bookstore concept created for a web design class project. It is imagined as a welcoming neighborhood space for readers, families, students, teachers, local writers, and people searching for thoughtful gifts.

The bookstore combines a thoughtfully selected book collection with stationery, gifts, and community programming.

What Makes Harbor Light Special

Thoughtful Book Collections

Sample categories include fiction, mystery, nonfiction, poetry, children's books, and literature inspired by Maine.

Creative Supplies

The stationery concept includes journals, notebooks, pens, and paper goods for writing, planning, and creative projects.

Community Connections

Example community activities include readings, children's story programs, and book discussions that bring readers together.

A Place for Readers

Harbor Light is imagined as more than a place to find books. It represents a welcoming space where readers can discover different perspectives, enjoy creative hobbies, and connect with others who share similar interests.

Whether someone enjoys a mystery novel, a children's story, a new journal, or a thoughtful gift, the bookstore concept celebrates the simple enjoyment of reading and creativity.
