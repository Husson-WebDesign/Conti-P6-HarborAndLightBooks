Our Purpose & Community Connection

Founded with a passion for literature and community connection, Harbor Light Books & Stationery serves as a warm, inviting independent bookstore in Bangor, Maine.

We believe a bookstore should be more than shelves of merchandise—it should be a neighborhood gathering space where readers discover new perspectives, local writers share their stories, and stationery enthusiasts find inspiration.

Curated Selection

Every book and stationery item on our shelves is thoughtfully chosen to spark curiosity, reflection, and joy.

Welcoming Readers

From lifelong bibliophiles to young readers just beginning their journey, everyone finds a welcoming seat here.

Community Focus

Proudly rooted in Bangor, we partner with regional authors and host regular community book discussions.
