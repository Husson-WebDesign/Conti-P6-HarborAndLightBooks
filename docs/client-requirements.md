## 2. Business Overview

Harbor Light Books & Stationery is an independent neighborhood bookstore serving readers, families, students, teachers, local writers, and gift buyers.

The store carries a carefully selected collection of books across several categories, with particular attention to Maine authors, fiction, mystery, children's books, and nonfiction.

In addition to books, the shop offers stationery, journals, greeting cards, and literary gifts.

Harbor Light Books & Stationery also serves as a community gathering place by hosting author readings, children's programs, book discussions, and events for local writers.

The business aims to feel welcoming and literary without appearing old-fashioned or overly formal.

## Avoid Images Showing

Do not use:

* recognizable large bookstore-chain branding,
* unrelated libraries presented as the store,
* antique-book-only imagery,
* dark academic-library imagery as the main theme,
* unrelated e-commerce warehouses,
* famous authors presented as store employees,
* or book covers copied from real copyrighted commercial books when fictitious titles are being used.

---

# 32. Content Restrictions

Students should not:

When organizing the website, use the following priority order:

1. Make Books & Gifts easy to find.
2. Make upcoming Events easy to discover.
3. Highlight Maine authors and community involvement.

6. Make store hours and contact information easy to locate.
7. Help customers understand special-order availability.
8. Maintain a cozy literary feeling without appearing old-fashioned.
